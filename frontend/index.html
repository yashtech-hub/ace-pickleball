What Ace does

A sideline coach built from video, not guesswork.

Ace combines player and ball detection, pose estimation, and a physics-based motion model to reconstruct a full pickleball rally — then explains it in plain language, the way a coach would after reviewing tape.

🎾

Gameplay Tracking

Detects both players and the ball frame-by-frame, reconstructing full court positioning and rally sequences.

🦴

Biomechanical Analysis

Pose estimation measures joint angles, swing paths, and reaction timing to surface real technique data.

🏓

Stroke Classification

A deep learning classifier labels every contact — dink, drive, volley, third-shot drop, smash — automatically.

🧭

Tactical Strategy Evaluation

Shot selection and court coverage are scored against rally outcomes to reveal patterns that win points.

📐

Physics-Based Motion Modeling

Ball trajectory, spin, and speed are reconstructed with a physics engine, not just pixel tracking.

🤖

Intelligent Coaching

Findings are translated into specific, prioritized recommendations — not just a wall of numbers.
